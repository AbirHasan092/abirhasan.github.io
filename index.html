Department of War: MURI · Aug. 2024 – Present

Implemented and analyzed Oscillator Ising Machines (OIM) in a CMOS integrated circuit platform for experimental demonstration, and demonstrated maximum-likelihood MIMO detection using coupled-oscillator computation.

Probabilistic-bit engines

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Nov. 2025 – Present

Designed a generalized probabilistic computing scheme with noisy bistable-latch circuits as tunable multi-state sampling engines, and modeled MTJ-based probabilistic bits with stochastic LLG simulations.

Intel · Sep. 2022 – Aug. 2024

Fabricated 30 nm-wide nanowires on epitaxially grown 2D materials and characterized resistivity, temperature coefficient of resistivity (TCR better than copper), and electromigration lifetime as alternatives to Cu back-end-of-line interconnects.

Circuit Design

  • Completed tapeout of a functional mixed-signal IC in TSMC 65 nm technology (1.5 mm², 1.2 V supply, 17 coupled oscillators), from circuit-block design and verification to full-custom layout, Calibre DRC/LVS/PEX sign-off, and post-layout tuning against extracted parasitics.
  • Designed and verified analog/mixed-signal blocks in Spectre and HSPICE with Verilog-A testbenches, process-corner and Monte Carlo analysis:
    • Designed a bandgap reference with multilevel bias generation and a Schmitt trigger buffer with tunable hysteresis.
    • Tuned a differential ring oscillator to 30 MHz; silicon frequency measured close to simulation.
    • Designed Pass-Transistor Logic programmable coupling for all 136 oscillator pairs, with 7 signed coupling-strength levels set by digital control bits.
  • Completed the full-chip layout, including placement of the TSMC-provided pad ring and ESD IP.
  • Configured an SPI interface with five 136-bit registers using digital synthesis and automation tools to meet custom connectivity and I/O protocol requirements.
  • Carried out post-silicon bring-up, testing, debugging, and characterization on custom PCB test boards to validate the fabricated chip against simulation.
  • Built a theoretical model for oscillator-based computing hardware using nonlinear dynamical analysis (phase-response, bifurcation, and stochastic-differential-equation methods), linking device noise, harmonic content, and coupling strength to circuit accuracy and guiding design choices that improve hardware performance.

Device Characterization and Modeling

  • Characterized fabricated devices on a probe station, measuring resistivity versus device width and across temperature and extracting a temperature coefficient of resistivity (TCR) better than copper.
  • Evaluated interconnect reliability through electromigration lifetime under accelerated stress and contact resistivity measurements, with data analysis in MATLAB and Python.
  • Modeled magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ) probabilistic bits with stochastic LLG (sLLG) simulations, including spin-orbit torque, and extracted switching-probability characteristics.

Process Technology

  • Fabricated 30 nm-wide nanowires on epitaxially grown 2D materials using a 30 kV E-beam lithography system (RAITH150 Two) to benchmark against Cu BEOL baselines.
  • Integrated the full device process flow (resist coating, E-beam exposure and development, metal deposition by E-beam evaporation or sputtering, lift-off, multi-layer alignment) and ran process-parameter sweeps to optimize it, checking results with SEM, AFM, profilometry, ellipsometry, and EDS.
  • Developed chlorine-based ICP-RIE dry-etch recipes (Oxford PlasmaPro 100 Cobra) for nanoscale pattern transfer; also performed wet processing (resist development, RCA cleaning, wet etches) and atomic layer deposition.

Coursework Projects
